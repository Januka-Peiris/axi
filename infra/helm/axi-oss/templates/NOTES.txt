To access AXI, set up port forwarding:

  API:
    kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "axi-oss.fullname" . }}-api {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
    Then open: http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}

  Frontend:
    kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "axi-oss.fullname" . }}-frontend {{ .Values.service.frontendPort }}:{{ .Values.service.frontendPort }}
    Then open: http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.frontendPort }}

{{- end }}

Health Check:
  curl http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/health
